Subject: Get ready for vibeCDA this weekend

Hi everyone,

vibeCDA starts Friday, November 13! Find the full schedule, registration, and
venue updates at https://vibecda.com. Building starts at 6pm Friday; code freeze
and submissions are due at noon Sunday. Awards are at 6pm Sunday. All times are
Pacific. Check the site for Sunday's venue confirmation.

Participant guide: https://vibecda.github.io/vibecda-starter/

Before you arrive:

1. Create GitHub and itch.io accounts, and have your chosen AI account ready.
2. Open the starter linked from the participant guide, publish it to your team's
   repository, and run npm run dev. Confirm you can play the game and use your AI
   tool. You can also use your own local tools or game engine.
3. Check remaining Codespaces and AI allowances. Bring your laptop and charger.

You can build solo or in a team of up to five. The Friday 4pm mixer is a chance to
find teammates. Freeplay and Wizards and Lizards are judged tracks; GTA Cda is an
unjudged shared community project.

Before the deadline, publish your itch.io project AND submit it to the appropriate
jam. The participant guide explains both steps. Include controls, credits, AI-use
disclosure, and a working build. Native games are welcome; identify their platform.

Freeplay: https://itch.io/jam/vibecda-2026-freeplay
Wizards and Lizards: https://itch.io/jam/vibecda-2026-wizards-and-lizards
Each track has five equally weighted judging criteria. Judging closes at 5:30pm
Sunday, before the 6pm awards.

Need help? Use the community link in the participant guide or ask a mentor at the
venue. We recommend getting your first build working before Friday evening.

See you there!
