Welcome to vibeCDA 2026: Freeplay

Build any game you want. Playability, learning, and fun come first.

Build a game with AI in a weekend in Coeur d'Alene, Idaho. Beginners welcome! This jam is for the Freeplay track.

Register and check the full rules, schedule, and venue updates at https://vibecda.com. Event registration and jam submission are separate steps. Participant guide: https://vibecda.github.io/vibecda-starter/

Build solo or in a team of up to five. Use any engine or development tools; browser games and downloadable native games are welcome. The Phaser/Vite/TypeScript starter is optional: https://github.com/VibeCdA/vibecda-starter

Bring your own AI account. Installed tools do not include subscriptions or API credits. Credit assets and disclose AI use. Only include code and assets you have permission to use.

Schedule — all times Pacific (PST / UTC−08:00)

Building starts Friday November 13, 2026 at 6pm.

Code freeze and submission deadline: Sunday November 15 at noon.

Judging closes Sunday at 5:30pm. Awards begin at 6pm.

Sunday open play is 2:30–5pm. Attendance at Sunday open play and awards is required for prizes under the event rules. The Sunday venue is currently to be confirmed; check vibecda.com for updates.

How to submit

Upload a working browser ZIP or downloadable game to a public, free itch.io project page. Add controls, supported platforms, screenshots, team information, credits, and AI disclosure. Include a demo video if judges may have difficulty running it. Then use this jam's Submit your project button and complete the fields. Uploading a project alone does not enter this jam. Save the submitted source commit/build before judging. New uploads are locked during judging; externally hosted demos are not frozen by this setting.

Judging

Only designated judges vote. Each criterion is scored 1–5, with equal weight (20% each):

• It's playable

• Fun factor

• Learning and growth

• Idea and personality

• Demo

The overall score averages these five criteria.

Tie policy: Learning and growth, then judges’ majority vote.

The host applies tie-breaks when declaring awards. Results remain hidden until announced by the host. GTA Cda is a separate, unjudged community build.
